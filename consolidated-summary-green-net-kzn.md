## ⚠️ Scope caveat (read first)

Although the research is titled **"Green Net KZN funding round investment," none of the ten retrieved sources mention an entity called "Green Net," its funding round, or any specific company's funding round.** What was actually retrieved is general material about KwaZulu-Natal municipal budgets, provincial development-finance institutions, and large investment pledges. Any claim tying these findings to "Green Net" would be an invention; none is made here.

## 1. KwaZulu-Natal municipal budget health, 2026/27 (South Africa Today, article dated 7 July 2026)

- KZN Finance MEC Francois Rodgers welcomed a sharp decline in unfunded municipal budgets for the 2026/27 cycle, framed as critical for sustainable service delivery.
- Per KZN Treasury assessments: of the 49 local and district councils that tabled and approved financial plans for 2026/27, **43 were certified fully funded**, compliant with Section 18 of the Municipal Finance Management Act (MFMA).
- This is an improvement over the prior cycle: after the 2025/26 adjusted budgets, **19 municipalities had adopted unfunded plans**; for 2026/27 that number fell to **six**. (Internally consistent: 43 funded + 6 unfunded = 49 tabled.)
- The six municipalities with unfunded 2026/27 budgets: **Mpofana; iMpendle; iNkosi Langalibalele; eMadlangeni; Ulundi Local Municipality; uThukela District Municipality.**
- Statuses still pending/undetermined:
  - **eNdumeni Local Municipality** — funding status undetermined because the council had not yet officially approved its budget at assessment time.
  - **Amajuba District Municipality** — reported adopting its budget on 29 June 2026; assessment ongoing.
  - **iNkosi Langalibalele Local Municipality** — appears both in the unfunded list *and* flagged as having re-tabled its 2026/27 budget on 25 June 2026, with the revised submission still awaiting reassessment (the source presents both facts; the listing predates the re-tabling outcome).
- Rodgers warned that passing unfunded budgets is a direct contravention of the MFMA and its regulations, undermines provincial financial stability, and compromises day-to-day service delivery; the Government of Provincial Unity will "act against non-compliance while supporting municipalities to improve."
- The Treasury committed to partnering with municipalities on financial-management frameworks and urged open communication so all councils develop credible, fully funded budgets.

## 2. KZN Growth Fund Agency (kzngrowthfund.co.za projects page + homepage + KZN EDTEA public-entities page)

Three sources describe the same institution, the **KZN Growth Fund Agency (KZNGFA)**:

- Vision: "to be KZN's leading Development Financier and Impact Investor" (EDTEA page, which lists it as a provincial public entity).
- Mandate/"INSPIRED INVESTING": accelerate Broad-Based Black Economic Empowerment (B-BBEE), job creation, and unlock economic growth opportunities in KZN (EDTEA page).
- Brand essence centres on "financing growth in the province" (homepage). Nature of business: project finance; Reg. No IT1437/2007/PMB; established August 2008; based at Kingsmead Office Park, Stamford Hill, Durban.
- Published project requirements:
  - Projects must have a sustainable economic impact in KZN and stimulate sustainable job creation and equitable distribution of wealth.
  - Minimum 40% BBBEE ownership or a BBBEE score of at least 65 points (level 4).
  - Funding is for capital expenditure (CAPEX) only, cash-flow based; **working capital is not financed**.
  - BEE shareholders must be operationally involved; promoter equity contribution minimum 10%.
  - Projects must be at an advanced implementation stage (disbursement within 6 months of application).
- Named portfolio/investment examples: iDube Cold Storage; Dark Fibre Africa (DFA); Southern African Shipyards; Link Africa; Mpact; HBM SA Health Protection Services; Microfinish; Ross Healthcare (Hillcrest Private Hospital and Gateway Private Hospital); Inoxa Industries; KwaDukuza Private Hospital.
- Site housekeeping: annual reports through 2024/25; current openings include a Chief Investment Officer post (June 2026) and two Investment Officer posts, plus a 24-month internship programme (January 2026); fraud-reporting hotline (0801 000 541 / Vuvuzela Hotline).
